Web Site Critiques

Questions:

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Jamilin

http://www.jamilin.com/

  1. My first impression was that the site looked very outdated. I was unimpressed with the low quality of the main banner image. I also was greeted with an ad within the first 5 seconds of clicking on the link.
  2. The website has a banner with some small links below it, and then 4 seperate images below that. Each image has a link above it, a paragraph below it, and a button to "FREE" tips below it.
  3. The site structure confuses me and it makes it hard for me to understand where I should even begin. I am unfamiliar with lot of the terms used and I have no idea what the services offered even are.
  4. The graphics and layout detract from the website due to how jaring and low quality some of the images are. The way its formated makes your eyes first look at the banner, and then the four images below it direct your attention to them right after. This lead me to miss all of the tabs listed right below the banner between them.
  5. The load time was ok for me. It did not take too long to load, but it took longer than most websites.
  6. Most of the text is readable except for the text in the colored tabs under the banner and the blue buttons listed at the bottom of the page. The banner is surprisingly readable due to the font being large and having a drop shadow. The main background of the webpage is white, so the black text and hyperlinks were readable.
  7. The site did not have a navigation bar. Navigating the site is hard due to relying on tabs and links.
  8. There is a slight multicolor theme seen in the website, with each color representing the different chakras. However, most pages are cluttered with images, youtube links, text, and hyperlinks that make the webpage too busy to notice any theme.
  9. The website was responsive to both small and narrow browser sizes.
  10. Personally I dont think the website was particularly well done. I thought it was unusual that accepted payment methods are one of the first things you see on the home screen. Every page felt cluttered and like it was just trying to sell me something.
